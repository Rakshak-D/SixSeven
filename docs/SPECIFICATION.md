# Slate product specification

Slate tracks construction-site progress, material, attendance and labour spending. The technical lead approved the repository baseline. This specification recovers the product rules from [commit d8aadaab](https://github.com/Rakshak-D/SixSeven/tree/d8aadaabe53233a724b421693ac6cdbb9f1f326d), specifically its root and database, server, shared and AI agent guides, API document and README. The official problem statement and external Claude artifacts were not consulted. Current AGENTS.md governs engineering workflow and ownership. [API.md](API.md) specifies the browser-facing contract.

## Architecture and roles

One Next.js App Router application with Hono at `/api/v1`, `@hono/zod-openapi`, shared browser-safe Zod, Drizzle/PostgreSQL 16, Better Auth and Vitest. Better Auth has its own cookie endpoint namespace. Membership is unique by project and user. An authenticated project creator receives engineer membership atomically. Contractors log stock, attendance, wages and corrections. Owners monitor progress and spending and decide proposals. Engineers log progress, draft and maintain checklists, and propose changes. Contractors may also log progress and submit proposals. Members can see project stock, timeline and activity, subject to financial restrictions. Return 404 for cross-project access, 403 for insufficient role within a visible project and 401 for missing sessions.

## Domain and integrity

- Projects have a name, location, start date, nonnegative budget and creator. Engineers invite owners and contractors by canonical email. The named account must authenticate and accept before gaining access. An invitation is single-use and unique by project and email. Existing contractor-created projects retain their direct enrollment flow and memberships. Materials have units, planned quantity, unit cost, tolerance and low stock threshold. Workers have category, active status and daily rate. Give forms a way to read materials and workers under the matching project access rules.
- Stock is a cached, nonnegative balance backed by append-only signed movements. Deliveries record supplier, challan, quantity and cost. Enforce one supplier/challan pair per project after normalization. Consumption and compensating decreases must use a guarded balance update in the same transaction as the movement and audit insert. Avoid read-before-decrement races.
- Store money in integer paise and quantity in numeric(14,3). Prices and budgets are nonnegative. Use positive even-paise daily rates to produce exact half-day wages. Attendance is unique by worker and date, supports 0/1/2 half-day units and snapshots the worker rate. Submit a day's batch atomically.
- Engineers may read the project attendance roster with worker names, dates and effective half-day units. Keep wage rates, amounts and adjustment details restricted to the owner and contractor.
- Attendance, wage payments, stock movements, adjustments and audit events are append-only. Future database constraints must prevent in-place changes. Corrections append a reason of at least 10 characters, effective prior and replacement values, session actor, target, and a compensating movement where applicable. This protects history at the database level. Cryptographic proof and blockchain are outside scope.
- Wage payments record wage versus advance, transfer mode and optional reference. Effective attendance uses the latest correction. Accrued = sum of effective half-day units × half the snapshotted rate. Due = accrued − paid wages − advances. Compute and return the report server-side. Use the effective amounts in reports. CSV exports must neutralize spreadsheet formulas.
- Summary includes budget, delivery spend, accrued wages and expenses, average spend per elapsed site day (minimum one day, rounded down to whole paise), material usage variance and stock alerts. Engineers get aggregate spending and operational metrics without a labour breakdown. Expenses contribute to reports. This baseline defines no expense write endpoint.
- Progress contains notes, optional checklist items and image attachments. Preserve session attribution in audited writes. Use project-scoped photo access and avoid exposing storage paths. Group timeline by IST calendar day. Return allowlisted activity summaries instead of raw audit payloads.
- Proposals carry title, description and cost impact. Only an owner can decide a pending proposal. Engineers generate editable checklist suggestions from a template or optional AI. A missing provider, timeout or invalid output falls back to a template. Save only after engineer review. Provider selection belongs to later implementation.
- Local demo data will provision synthetic owner, contractor and engineer accounts, a working demo project and a separate project to verify isolation. No demo credentials or live user data belong in source.

### M6 checklist verification and history

Frontend requests #7 and #8 are approved. A contractor starts a task (todo → doing) and submits a note and private photos (doing or sent_back → waiting). An engineer accepts (waiting → done) or sends it back with a reason of at least ten characters (waiting → sent_back). Owners can read the evidence and history. All transitions and reviews record the actor and time. Engineer additions, renames and soft removals preserve prior values, reasons and evidence. Items awaiting review cannot be removed. Proposal decisions never change budgets or execute payments. M6 uses deterministic templates. External AI provider selection remains deferred.
